A quiet little toolkit

Everything lives in small cream-and-brown windows that sit on top of your work, and Cinder looks after them.

Focus timer

Pomodoro rounds or a countdown of your own, with a gentle chime when it’s time to rest.

Stopwatch

Count up with start, pause and reset. Handy for studying or timing a stretch of deep work.

To-do list

Add tasks, tick them off, and Cinder pops up to celebrate. Your list is saved for next time.

Water reminders

A nudge as often as you like, and she can hop over your other windows to deliver it.

She follows your cursor with her eyes. Circle your mouse around her and she purrs. Leave her alone for a while and she curls up to sleep.

How to use Cinder

A short manual, so you can find everything she does.

  1. Start herOpen Cinder and she appears on your desktop, always on top of your other windows.
  2. Move herClick and drag her anywhere you like. She stays where you leave her.
  3. Open the menuRight-click her to find the timer, stopwatch, to-do list and the other tools.
  4. QuitRight-click her and choose Exit when you’re done for the day.

Timer & Pomodoro

  • Right-click Cinder and open the timer.
  • Pick a Pomodoro round or set your own countdown.
  • Press start, and a chime tells you when it’s time to rest.

Stopwatch

  • Open it from the right-click menu.
  • Start counts up, pause holds your time, and reset takes it back to zero.

To-do list

  • Open it from the menu and type a task to add it.
  • Tick a task off and confetti pops, with Cinder celebrating.
  • Your list is saved, so it’s still there next time.

Water reminders

  • Open the water settings from the menu.
  • Choose how often you’d like a nudge.
  • Cinder reminds you, even if other windows are open.

Petting & moods

  • Circle your cursor around her three times to pet her. She smiles and sends up tiny hearts.
  • Her eyes follow your cursor while you work.
  • If you leave her alone for a while, she curls up and naps. Click her to wake her.

Windows

  • Tool windows open right where Cinder is sitting.
  • Close one with the cross, or minimise it to keep it out of the way.
  • You can have several open at once.

Good to know

Where do I find the tools?

Right-click Cinder. Every tool opens from that menu.

I lost her! Where did she go?

She always stays on top, so check the edges of your screens (and any second monitor), as you may have dragged her far away. If she’s still missing, close and reopen Cinder.

Windows says it doesn’t recognise the app.

Choose More info, then Run anyway. Cinder isn’t code-signed yet, which is why the warning appears.

She doesn’t react to my clicks on a Mac.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and allow Cinder.

Something isn’t working.

Please tell me on GitHub and I’ll take a look.

Bring her home

Download, open, and she’s on your desktop.

Windows

  1. Download Cinder.exe.
  2. Double-click it to start Cinder.
  3. If a blue SmartScreen box appears, choose More info, then Run anyway. Cinder isn’t code-signed yet.
  4. Right-click her to open the menu.
Download for Windows

macOS

  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Right-click Cinder.app, choose Open, then Open again. It isn’t signed with Apple yet.
  3. If she doesn’t wake on clicks, allow Cinder in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility.
  4. The Mac version is brand new. Found a bug? Tell me on GitHub.
